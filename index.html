Trip Highlights

My recent trip to Fire Island National Seashore was an unforgettable escape from the bustle of city life. From the moment I stepped onto the quiet, car-free shores, I knew I had found something special. I spent my days hiking the Sunken Forest Trail, spotting deer among the ancient holly trees, and watching the sunset paint the Atlantic sky in shades of orange and pink. The historic Fire Island Lighthouse offered breathtaking views of the barrier island, and the local seafood shacks served up the freshest clams I've ever tasted. Whether you're a nature lover, a photographer, or just need a peaceful retreat, Fire Island delivers on every level.
